About The Founder

A Journey Built With Passion, Creativity & Perseverance

CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI is the Founder & CEO of SRIHAANFILMS — a passionate filmmaker, cinematographer, editor, producer, and photographer whose journey in cinema reflects dedication, creativity, and perseverance.

With a deep love for visual storytelling, he began his career working in different areas of filmmaking and gained experience as a Cinematographer, Editor, and Camera Professional.

He worked as the Cinematographer for the film “Manasu Palike Mouna Ragam”, showcasing his strong visual style and cinematic vision.

Though several projects faced unexpected challenges and remained incomplete midway, his passion for cinema never stopped.

Manasu Palike Mouna Ragam Telugu Short Film

MANASU PALIKE MOUNA RAGAM is an emotional musical project presented by Balaji Productions, crafted with heartfelt storytelling, soulful music, and visually rich cinematic presentation.

The project is produced by Suresh Shetkar under the banner of Balaji Productions. The complete creative vision including Story, Lyrics, Screenplay, Dialogues, and Direction has been handled by Sainath Reddy, whose storytelling beautifully captures emotions and human relationships.

The visual aesthetics of the project have been brought to life by CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI, who worked as the Cinematographer & Editor, delivering cinematic depth and emotional visuals throughout the film.

The soulful music for MANASU PALIKE MOUNA RAGAM is composed by Krishna, with keyboard arrangements by Babu. The songs are beautifully rendered by singer Sadashiva, adding emotional power and musical charm to the project.

Prabhakar served as the Co-Director, while Shivashanker and Kashinatham worked as Assistant Cameramen, supporting the visual execution of the production.

The promotional poster designs were creatively developed by Srihaancolours and Sainath Reddy, giving the project a distinctive visual identity.

The film is proudly supported by media partner TG9FM, helping the project connect with wider audiences through promotional and digital media platforms.

MANASU PALIKE MOUNA RAGAM promises a touching cinematic experience filled with music, emotions, silence, and meaningful storytelling.

Best Photogrphy Award

CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI successfully received a prestigious participation certification at the 2025 Journey Towards Photography Conference & Workshop held in Hyderabad.

The Journey Towards Photography is a recognized Photo & Video Conference and Workshop that provides professional-level training and hands-on experience for photography enthusiasts, filmmakers, and visual artists.

The conference focused on important photography and cinematography techniques including lighting setups, camera settings, composition, and professional visual storytelling methods. The workshop was designed for both beginners and experienced professionals looking to improve their creative and technical skills.

CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI actively participated in the workshop sessions and received an official Photography Participation Certificate in recognition of his dedication and involvement in the field of photography and cinematography.

The certification reflects his passion for visual arts, filmmaking, and continuous learning in modern photography techniques. His participation in the Hyderabad conference further strengthens his creative journey and professional experience in cinematography and media production.

The 2025 Journey Towards Photography Conference & Workshop Hyderabad served as an inspiring platform for photographers and filmmakers to connect, learn, and enhance their artistic vision through practical training and industry-level guidance.

Best Cinematographer Award by Rudra Best Helping Foundation & Rudra TV

CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI, a talented cinematographer from the Zaheerabad region, received the prestigious Golden Nandi Award for excellence in cinematography.

CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI, who belongs to Gurujuwada Village in Kohir Mandal of the Zaheerabad region, was recognized with the Best Cinematographer Award for his outstanding contribution to cinematography and visual storytelling.

The award was presented in recognition of his exceptional creative vision, cinematic framing, and artistic excellence in capturing visuals that bring stories to life on screen. His dedication and passion for cinematography have earned him appreciation and recognition in the creative industry.

Programs like these, aimed at encouraging local talent and recognizing artistic achievements, continue to inspire young artists and filmmakers to pursue their dreams and contribute to the world of cinema and visual arts.

The achievement of CHANDRASHEKAR GIDDALAMARI receiving the Golden Nandi Award has become a proud moment for the people of the Zaheerabad region and stands as a symbol of inspiration for aspiring cinematographers and artists.
